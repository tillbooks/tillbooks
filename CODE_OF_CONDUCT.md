# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in the TILL community a
respectful, harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or
invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or
identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Behaviour that keeps this community worth being in:

- Being kind to people and rigorous about code. Those are different targets.
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback gracefully.
- Assuming the other person is trying to help, especially when they are wrong.
- Accepting responsibility, apologising to those affected by our mistakes, and learning from them.
- Focusing on what is best for the community, not for any one contributor.

Behaviour that is not acceptable:

- Sexualised language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without their
  explicit permission.
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This Code applies in all community spaces, including the repository, issues, pull requests, and
discussions, and it applies when an individual is officially representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behaviour to **hello@tillbooks.ch**. All
complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. The maintainers will respect the
privacy and security of the reporter of any incident.

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and will take fair
corrective action in response to behaviour they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or
harmful. That can include editing or removing contributions, and temporary or permanent bans.
