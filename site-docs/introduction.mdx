TILL (Trusted Independent Ledger Library) is an agent-first ERP for Swiss small businesses. It is
open source under MIT and costs nothing. It runs on your own computer or on a server you host yourself, and your
books live in a single SQLite file you can copy, back up and read without asking anyone's permission.

It covers accounting and MWST, invoices with the QR-bill, supplier bills and payment files, bank
statements, contacts, products and stock, projects and time tracking, documents and fixed assets.
One install can hold several companies.

Every function is exposed as an MCP tool, so your AI agent works through the same actions as the
Studio, the app you work in, and the Studio shows you what the agent did. It works with Claude
Desktop, Cursor and other MCP clients that can start a local stdio server. By default, the agent
waits for your OK before it books anything, moves your money, grants access or deletes your data
for good: it drafts, and you approve. For each of those areas you decide whether it may act on its
own. Much of what you can change back, such as drafts and contact details, runs without asking, and
every call the agent makes is recorded. The dial covers agents that connect to TILL. An agent that
can drive your browser or run commands on your computer can act as you: give your agent TILL, not
your browser or your shell.

## Status

TILL is in public beta, version 1.0.0-beta.1. Nomadik GmbH, the company behind TILL, keeps its own
books in it. If you move your books to TILL, keep your old system running until the figures match:
the parallel run in the migration area compares them to the Rappen. The
[quickstart](/quickstart) gets you to a running Studio.

## What TILL is built for

Switzerland, specifically:

- Swiss QR-bill (QR-Rechnung) with the structured-address standard
- MWST at 8.1%, 3.8% and 2.6%, the effective and the Saldosteuersatz method, and the
  MWST-Abrechnung with its eCH-0217 file for the ESTV ePortal
- Kontenrahmen KMU, seeded out of the box
- ISO 20022 banking: camt.053 statements in, pain.001 payment files out
- German (de-CH) and English

## What the beta does not do yet

- No payroll. TILL exports your employee data for your payroll provider and posts the wage journal
  you bring back. Pay slips, AHV and Swissdec stay with the provider.
- No MWST filing. TILL prepares the MWST-Abrechnung and writes the eCH-0217 file. You upload it and
  file in the ESTV ePortal yourself. See [MWST](/swiss/mwst).
- No MWST on received consideration (vereinnahmte Entgelte). TILL computes the Abrechnung on agreed
  consideration (vereinbarte Entgelte) and refuses a period set up the other way.
- No email sending yet. TILL makes the PDF of an invoice, quote or reminder, and you send it from
  your own mail program. Sending through your own mail account is planned for a later beta release.
- German (de-CH) and English only. No French or Italian yet.
- No mobile app, and no Windows app. We do not test on Windows; the documented route there is
  [running TILL inside an agent](/mcp/run-inside-agent).
- No direct bank connection in this release. The EBICS client is built and has been tested with
  ZKB's test platform, but the module that connects it to the bank is not published yet (it will
  be, under MIT).
  Until then you download camt.053 statements from your eBanking and import them. Payments are
  always a pain.001 file that you upload and release in your eBanking.
- On your own computer, backups run when you start them, not on a schedule. Keep a copy somewhere
  other than the computer the books are on. (The [self-hosting recipe](/self-hosting/under-an-hour)
  replicates the book continuously.)
- Several people in one book only on a server you host yourself (see
  [self-hosting](/self-hosting/under-an-hour)). The local app is for one computer.
- No eBill sending, e-signature or online customer portal.
