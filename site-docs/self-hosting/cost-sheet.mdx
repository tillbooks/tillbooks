<Note>
  Every price below was read on 2026-09-06 from the provider's own page or a dated review.
  Re-verify before buying. Providers change plans quietly: Hetzner repriced its cloud range in
  2026, and free tiers come and go, so treat any "free" line as something to confirm, not to lean on.
</Note>

A TILL instance is small. The ledger of a ten-person company is a few megabytes and the image is
442 MB. On the drill stack, TILL, Caddy, oauth2-proxy and Litestream idled at about 85 MB of
memory together (Pocket ID was not part of that measurement). Any host on this page is oversized
for it. Pick by residency, and by how much you trust a free tier to stay free.

The served host is provider-neutral (D124): the recipe runs on any small VM, and the compose file
is identical everywhere. The recommendation is Swiss first, Infomaniak for compute and for the
backup bucket, so the whole instance sits under one account you already use for mail and DNS.

| Host | What you get | Price | Swiss | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|

| Hetzner CAX11 | Ampere ARM, 2 vCPU, 4 GB, 40 GB NVMe | about EUR 4.49 per month without a public IPv4, about EUR 4.99 with one | no (Falkenstein, Nuremberg, Helsinki) | The cheap non-Swiss ARM box. You pick it for the price, not the residency. |
| Fly.io shared-cpu-1x, 256 MB | one Machine, one image | about USD 2 per month, plus USD 0.15 per GB of volume | no (Amsterdam is the nearest) | No free tier since 2024. Fly does not run compose: the stack would need one Machine per container. The fallback. |
| Your Mac behind a tunnel | whatever it is | CHF 0 | your desk | A laptop is not a server. Useful for a demo, not for the books. |

## Backup bucket

Any S3-compatible endpoint works. Swiss first: Infomaniak Swiss Backup.

| Bucket | Price | Swiss | Endpoint line in `.env.example` |
|---|---|---|---|
| Infomaniak Swiss Backup (S3, recommended) | from CHF 2.40 per month for 200 GB; a 2026 price roundup lists the S3 cloud mode at about CHF 4.20, so budget for that until you hold Infomaniak's own quote | yes | `s3.swiss-backup03.infomaniak.com` (cluster number and region from the Manager) |
| Cloudflare R2 | 10 GB free, no egress fees | no | `<account-id>.r2.cloudflarestorage.com` |
| Backblaze B2 (S3) | first 10 GB free | no | `s3.<region>.backblazeb2.com` (region is the second label of the endpoint) |
| Tigris | first 5 GB free, no egress fees | no | `fly.storage.tigris.dev` |

Litestream ships WAL pages every second. A ledger that books fifty entries a day produces a few
megabytes of segments a day; retention keeps that bounded. On a metered bucket, request count is
the line worth watching, and only on a busy instance; raise `sync-interval` in `litestream.yml` if
you ever see it.

## Two worked totals

The Swiss-first recommendation (D124): Infomaniak VPS Lite for compute, Infomaniak Swiss Backup for
the bucket, Pocket ID, one DNS record at the registrar you already pay. About CHF 5 to 10 per month
before the seat price (CHF 2.70 for the smallest VPS Lite, CHF 7.20 for 2 vCPU and 4 GB, plus the
Swiss Backup line), one vendor, Swiss end to end.

The cheap non-Swiss variant on the same recipe: Hetzner CAX11 (about EUR 4.99 per month with a
public IPv4) plus Cloudflare R2 or Backblaze B2 for the bucket (both free at TILL's size). That
trims a few francs and moves the host and the replica out of Switzerland; pick it only when
residency does not matter to the customer.

## What is not on this sheet

Seat pricing for a managed instance (D107, out of repo), an attorney's pass on hosting terms,
and the cost of your own time. The last one dominates: the recipe is an hour the first time and
ten minutes the third.
