TILL handles Swiss MWST at the rates in force since 1 January 2024:

| Rate | Applies to |
| --- | --- |
| 8.1% | The standard rate (Normalsatz) |
| 3.8% | Accommodation (Sondersatz für Beherbergung) |
| 2.6% | The reduced rate (reduzierter Satz), for food, books, medicine and similar |

## Abrechnung

TILL prepares the MWST-Abrechnung from the ledger rather than from a separate tally, so the return
and the books cannot disagree. It handles the effective method and the Saldosteuersatz method, on
agreed consideration (vereinbarte Entgelte). A checklist per MWST period lists what to check before
you file.

TILL does not compute the Abrechnung on received consideration (vereinnahmte Entgelte) yet. If you
account for MWST that way, TILL refuses the period rather than hand you figures on the wrong basis.

## Filing

TILL writes the Abrechnung as an eCH-0217 file (version 2.0.0), the XML format the ESTV ePortal
imports. You upload that file in the ePortal, check the figures there, and submit. TILL does not file
for you.

On the Saldosteuersatz method, record the Tätigkeitscodes the ESTV approved for you (you find them in
the ePortal under «Abrechnungsmodalitäten»). Without them TILL does not write the file, because the
ESTV accepts only approved codes; you then enter the figures in the ePortal by hand.
