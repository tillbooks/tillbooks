---

TILL handles Swiss MWST at the rates in force since 1 January 2024:

| Rate | Applies to |
| --- | --- |
| 8.1% | The standard rate (Normalsatz) |
| 3.8% | Accommodation (Sondersatz für Beherbergung) |
| 2.6% | The reduced rate (reduzierter Satz), for food, books, medicine and similar |

## Abrechnung

TILL prepares the MWST-Abrechnung from the ledger rather than from a separate tally, so the return
and the books cannot disagree. Both the effective method and the Saldosteuersatz method are in the
specs.
