The QR-bill (QR-Rechnung) replaced the old orange and red Einzahlungsscheine. TILL generates the
Swiss QR Code with the structured address format (S), which has been the required form since the
combined-address format was retired.

## What it covers

- The scannable Swiss QR Code, drawn on the invoice PDF
- QR-IBAN with the QR reference (QRR), and a plain IBAN with a Creditor Reference (SCOR). Every
  QR-bill TILL draws carries one of the two; TILL never uses the "no reference" type.
- Structured creditor and debtor addresses

TILL puts the Swiss QR Code on the invoice PDF, with the invoice number and the amount as text. The
creditor, debtor and reference are inside the QR code but not yet printed beside it, and the full
payment part with the receipt, laid out to the SIX style guide, is not drawn yet. TILL does not send the invoice either: you send the PDF from your own mail program.

<Note>
  Public beta. Before your first real invoices go out, scan one QR-bill with your own banking app and
  check the account, the amount and the reference it shows.
</Note>
