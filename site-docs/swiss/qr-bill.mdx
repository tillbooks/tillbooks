---
title: "Swiss QR-bill"
description: "QR-Rechnung with the structured-address standard."
---

The QR-bill (QR-Rechnung) replaced the old orange and red Einzahlungsscheine. TILL generates the
Swiss QR Code payment part with the structured address format (S), which has been the required form
since the combined-address format was retired.

## What it covers

- The Swiss QR Code payment part and receipt
- QR-IBAN with the QR reference (QRR), and IBAN with a Creditor Reference (SCOR) or no reference
- Structured creditor and debtor addresses

<Note>
  Pre-alpha: specified and in progress, not shipped. Do not use TILL for real invoices yet.
</Note>
